    ABOUT MARGIN

    A front page you can keep.

    Each edition preserves the top 100 stories from the official HN top-stories feed. Morning and evening slots use UTC. Actual capture time is shown on each edition.

    Save an edition to this device before going offline. Readable article text, supported PDF and image files, and up to 50 comments in HN thread order are included. Comment scores are not public. Media, paywalls, and pages requiring sign-in may not be available.

    Past editions appear as they are collected; historical front pages cannot be reconstructed. The calendar shows the latest 10 days. All captured editions are retained on the server and available in All saved editions. Password recovery does not delete them. Bookmarks, saved comments, and read status are device-local.

    On iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Choose Open as Web App if shown.

    Downloaded editions remain readable offline on this device. Use your device passcode to protect them.

    Reading and downloading reuse server copies. The collector uses public APIs, caches saved articles, and pauses when a source requests a slowdown.

    Your recovery checklist

    Save your password and latest recovery code in your password manager. Each password change issues a new code and invalidates the old one. We cannot display your old recovery code.

    Recover with your code · Change password & get a new code
    Test on your iPhone or iPad
    1. Open Margin in Safari, add it to your Home Screen, and launch that installed app.
    2. Save an edition there. Use “Check offline readiness” and confirm its article/comment counts.
    3. Enable airplane mode and disable Wi-Fi. Close and reopen Margin from the Home Screen.
    4. Open a saved article, a discussion, and a saved PDF/image if available.
    5. Reconnect and check the archive status. Repeat after an iOS update or clearing browser data.

    A browser readiness check cannot certify airplane mode or iOS storage retention.
